As easy as conditioning

No mixing bowl, no developer, no hour-long wait.

Shampoo

Wash as usual and squeeze out excess water. It also works on dry hair for stubborn greys.

Apply and wait

Work it through the grey areas with gloves and leave it on for about 10 minutes.

Rinse

Rinse well with lukewarm water. Repeat a few times to build colour, then once or twice a week.

Please do a patch test 48 hours before first use, even with gentle formulas.

What we are leaving out

Most box dyes rely on PPD, ammonia and peroxide to force colour inside the hair. Our treatment is being developed to colour the surface of grey hair instead, gently and gradually.
